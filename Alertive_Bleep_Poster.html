Full Format.

Full format

66NUH
81RLH

Phone bleeps now also alert Alertive — check the app as well as your Bleep.

How to Dial.

  • Dial all 11 digits: site prefix + Bleep number + extension, in full.
  • Use the prefix for the site you are calling — 66, 81, 51 or 71.
  • Save the full number in your contacts to get it right, always.
